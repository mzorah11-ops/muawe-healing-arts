04 About us

Local welders. Real experience.

Specialist Welding Services UK is based on Moss Bank Road in Widnes. With over 10 years of industry experience, we handle everything from small repairs to custom fabrication.

We offer both workshop-based and fully mobile welding, so you never have to work around us. We work with ferrous and non-ferrous metals, and we’re happy to talk through your project before any work starts.
